Provenance and limitations

All animated joint angles and graphed measurements come from OpenModelica-generated CSV data embedded in this file. The arm's fixed geometry matches the authored M0 Modelica source and is shown as an XY projection, not as OMEdit's full 3D visualization. This preview cannot infer new physics or arbitrary Modelica scene geometry. For genuine native 3D MultiBody animation, open the model in OMEdit and select Simulate with Animation.
